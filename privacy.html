Privacy and analytics

Useful information. Proportionate collection.

This website does not use customer accounts or transmit the fit-request form. When you submit it, your own email application opens a prepared message that you can review before sending. Optional analytics remain off unless you actively accept them.

Information you send

If you contact Put It On The Wall by email, the information you provide is used to respond, assess fit and maintain an appropriate record of the commercial conversation.

Analytics choices

With permission, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and useful actions. Analytics does not load before acceptance. Your choice is stored in your browser.

TechnologyPurposeWhen used
Local storageRemembers whether you accepted or declined analytics.When you make a choice.
Google Analytics 4Provides aggregated information about site use and enquiry actions.Only after you accept.

Legal basis and retention

Enquiry information is processed to take steps at your request before entering a contract and for legitimate interests in managing genuine business relationships. Optional analytics use consent. Information is retained only while relevant or legally necessary.

Sharing and international processing

Your information is not sold. Necessary service providers may process it for email, hosting or analytics, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, or withdraw consent for optional analytics.
