04 / The WALL method

Four moves.
One shared reality.

The WALL method turns scattered knowledge into a visible path from evidence to action.

  1. W
    Witness reality

    Put the truth in the room.

    Evidence, experience, constraints, contradictions and unknowns—without forcing them into a premature story.

  2. A
    Articulate the problem

    Name what is actually happening.

    Separate symptoms from causes, inherited assumptions from facts and activity from value.

  3. L
    Locate the leverage

    Find where change will move the system.

    Compare interventions against the evidence. Technology is an option, never the starting point.

  4. L
    Launch the move

    Turn understanding into commitment.

    One decision, one owner, one proof rule and the next action visible to everyone carrying the risk.

11 / Questions

The sceptical executive's questions.

Is this a workshop?

No. A workshop may be one moment in the work. The product is shared understanding, a defensible decision and the infrastructure to act on it.

Do you produce a slide deck?

No strategy theatre. Executive outputs are concise decision records, prioritised registers, digitised walls and action plans designed to be used.

Does AI Opportunity Wall always recommend AI?

No. Pursue, fix, defer and stop are all legitimate outcomes. A credible no-AI decision can save more value than another pilot.

Who needs to be involved?

An accountable executive sponsor, one capable internal operator and the small cross-functional group that holds the evidence and operating reality.

What does it cost?
